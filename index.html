How the flags work

South African municipalities run on a 1 July to 30 June financial year. The tool compares each line's share of budget spent with the share of the year that has passed.

Over budget
Spend has passed the adjusted budget.
Ahead of pace
Spend is more than 10 percentage points above the year elapsed.
Behind pace
Spend is more than 15 percentage points below the year elapsed. Persistent underspending can mean delayed projects or unspent conditional grants.
On pace
Anything else.
